An index of articles, guides and reported rulings on Indian law as it applies to Non-Resident Indians, OCI cardholders, foreign nationals and foreign companies.

In Short

This page collects the articles, guides and reported rulings on this site that concern Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), Overseas Citizens of India (OCI), foreign nationals and foreign companies dealing with Indian law. It is a reading list organised by subject. The material is general information, stated as on the date shown on each item, and is not legal advice.
