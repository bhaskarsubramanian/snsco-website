About

Deba Prasad Guha brings over three decades of regulatory experience in India's food safety administration. Across a career spanning the Ministry of Food Processing Industries and the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI), he rose from Inspector to Director, implementing the Fruit Products Order, 1955 and the Food Safety &amp; Standards Act, 2006 across postings in Lucknow, Mumbai, Kolkata and Delhi.

At FSSAI he served as Designated Officer for the Eastern and North Eastern regions and as Authorised Officer for food import clearance at Kolkata Sea Port and Airport, and represented India at international forums on food safety cooperation — including SAARC GMP/GHP/HACCP standard-setting in Pakistan and a bilateral food safety arrangement with New Zealand. He is a member of the firm's advisory panel on regulatory compliance under the FSS Act and allied food safety regulations.
