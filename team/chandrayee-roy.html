About

Chandrayee Roy is an associate based in Kolkata. She took her law degree in 2022. Her areas of practice include commercial disputes and civil litigation, including matters before the Barasat District Court.

She appears before the Calcutta High Court, the City Civil Court and tribunals including the NCLT and DRT.
