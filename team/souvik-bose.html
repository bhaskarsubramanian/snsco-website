About

Souvik Bose is an associate based at the firm's Kolkata office. He took his law degree in 2013. His areas of practice include corporate-commercial agreements, media law and civil litigation.

He appears before the Calcutta High Court, local civil courts and administrative tribunals; his areas of work include intellectual property rights management and commercial transactions.
