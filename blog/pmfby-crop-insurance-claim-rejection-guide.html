Filing a PMFBY Crop Insurance Claim: Process, Timelines and Common Rejection Grounds

A practical guide for a farmer or farmer-collective dealing with a Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana claim, covering the loss-intimation window, common documentary rejection grounds, and the appeal route: a companion to this site's existing PMFBY-related case coverage (data-entry-error denials) and its ENWR/agricultural-commodities guide, focused specifically on the claims process itself.

By S&S Co. Advocates & Solicitors · Published 28 September 2026 · Informational content, not legal advice: see our disclaimer

Overview

A practical guide for a farmer or farmer-collective dealing with a Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana claim, covering the loss-intimation window, common documentary rejection grounds, and the appeal route: a companion to this site's existing PMFBY-related case coverage (data-entry-error denials) and its ENWR/agricultural-commodities guide, focused specifically on the claims process itself.

What You Need to Know

  • A farmer must intimate crop loss within 72 hours of the loss event through the insurer, the toll-free helpline, the Crop Insurance App, or the local agriculture/revenue department, missing this window is the single most common reason claims are rejected, regardless of the actual extent of loss
  • Claims are also commonly rejected for data-mismatch reasons entirely outside the farmer's control at the time of the loss: incorrect IFSC or Aadhaar-bank seeding, sowing/crop data entered incorrectly at enrolment, outdated Khasra/Khatauni land records, and name mismatches across Aadhaar, bank passbook and land records
  • Premium must be paid, and enrolment completed, before the state- and crop-specific cut-off date: this date varies by season, state, district and notified crop, so a farmer should confirm the applicable date locally rather than assuming a fixed national deadline
  • Yield-based claims are settled using Crop Cutting Experiment (CCE) data at the notified insurance unit (village/village-panchayat) level, while 'localised risk' claims (for events like hailstorm, landslide and inundation) are assessed on an individual, farm-level basis and require prompt on-site intimation and inspection
  • A farmer whose claim is rejected can raise a grievance through the insurance company's own grievance cell, the PMFBY National Grievance Portal, or the district-level Grievance Redressal Committee, and courts have shown willingness to direct disbursal where rejection was based on an administrative or data-entry error rather than a genuine absence of loss
  • Because disbursal is routed through the Direct Benefit Transfer system into an Aadhaar-seeded bank account, a farmer should proactively verify that bank and Aadhaar details are correctly linked well before a loss event occurs, rather than discovering a mismatch only when a claim is processed

Frequently Asked Questions

Within how long must a farmer report a crop loss under PMFBY?

Within 72 hours of the loss event, through the insurer, the toll-free helpline, the Crop Insurance App, or the local agriculture/revenue office, missing this window is the most common reason claims are rejected.

Can a claim be rejected over a bank data-entry error rather than the actual loss?

It can be initially, but courts have shown willingness to direct disbursal where rejection was based on an administrative or bank data-entry error on the National Crop Insurance Portal rather than a genuine absence of loss.

What can a farmer do if a genuine claim is rejected?

Raise a grievance through the insurer's own grievance cell, the PMFBY National Grievance Portal, or the District-Level Grievance Redressal Committee.
