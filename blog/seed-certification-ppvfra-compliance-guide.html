Seed Certification and PPV&FR Act Compliance: A Guide for Seed Companies and Farmers

A guide explaining India's seed certification system alongside the Protection of Plant Varieties and Farmers' Rights Act, covering what registration protects, the scope of the farmers' seed-saving exemption, and common compliance pitfalls for seed businesses.

By S&S Co. Advocates & Solicitors · Published 29 September 2026 · Informational content, not legal advice — see our disclaimer

Overview

A guide explaining India's seed certification system alongside the Protection of Plant Varieties and Farmers' Rights Act, covering what registration protects, the scope of the farmers' seed-saving exemption, and common compliance pitfalls for seed businesses.

What You Need to Know

  • India operates a largely voluntary seed certification system under the Seeds Act, 1966 and the Seeds Rules, focused on verifying the genetic purity, physical purity and germination standards of seed sold as 'certified' or 'truthfully labelled' seed — this is distinct from, and can operate alongside, protection of a plant variety itself under the Protection of Plant Varieties and Farmers' Rights (PPV&FR) Act, 2001
  • Registration of a plant variety under the PPV&FR Act gives the breeder exclusive rights to produce, sell, market, distribute, import or export propagating material of that variety, but the Act contains a specific farmers' rights exemption permitting a farmer to save, use, sow, re-sow, exchange, share or sell their own farm-produced seed of a registered variety, provided the seed is not sold in a branded package bearing the registered variety's name
  • The distinction between a farmer's protected seed-saving/informal exchange and unauthorised commercial resale of a registered variety's branded propagating material has been a recurring point of dispute — breeders and seed traders should understand that this exemption protects the farmer's own informal practice, not a trader's branded commercial resale of the breeder's variety
  • A seed company seeking to sell certified seed of a PPV&FR-registered variety generally needs both a seed certification/truthful-labelling compliance process under the Seeds Act framework and, separately, either its own breeder registration or a valid licence from the registered breeder — one without the other leaves a gap in the company's legal position
  • Common compliance pitfalls include marketing seed as 'certified' without actually routing it through the applicable state seed certification agency's field-inspection and lab-testing process, and selling propagating material of a PPV&FR-registered variety without a valid licence from the breeder, even where the seed itself meets certification quality standards
  • Because seed certification is largely state-administered while PPV&FR registration and enforcement operate at the central level through the PPV&FR Authority, a seed business operating across multiple states should track both compliance tracks separately for each variety and each state of operation

Frequently Asked Questions

Are seed certification and PPV&FR variety registration the same thing?

No — certification under the Seeds Act verifies seed quality (genetic and physical purity, germination), while PPV&FR registration protects the breeder's exclusive commercial rights in the variety itself; a seed business generally needs to address both separately.

Can a farmer sell their own saved seed of a registered variety?

Generally yes, under the PPV&FR Act's farmers' rights exemption for saving, using, exchanging and selling farm-produced seed — but not if the seed is sold in a branded package bearing the registered variety's name, which is treated as commercial resale outside the exemption.

Is seed certification under the Seeds Act mandatory in India?

It is largely a voluntary system for most crops (with truthfully-labelled seed as an alternative compliance route), though specific notifications can make certification compulsory for certain seed categories — this should be checked against the applicable current notification.
