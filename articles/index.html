Arbitration & Dispute Resolution 📖

Arbitration in India After Balasamy, CORE and the Stamp Act Ruling: Where the Law Stands Today

Between December 2023 and April 2025 the Supreme Court decided, through three separate Constitution Benches, questions that had divided High Courts and earlier Supreme Court benches for a decade: whether an unstamped contract can carry a live arbitration clause, whether one party can control how the arbitrator is chosen, and whether a court can modify an award rather than only set it aside. Each answer narrows what used to be a wide field of threshold objections, though none of the three rulings closes every door it touches.

The IBC Amendment Act, 2026: What Actually Changes for Lenders, Operational Creditors and Promoters

The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (Amendment) Act, 2026 received Presidential assent on 6 April 2026 and most of its provisions were brought into force from 26 May 2026. It settles, by statute, an admission-stage argument the Supreme Court had been working through for four years, creates a creditor-driven route into insolvency, and gives a failed process one more chance before liquidation. Its most discussed reforms, on group and cross-border insolvency, are not yet operative, and that distinction matters for anyone advising on a live matter.
